# Requests of the people about their data (GDPR art. 12-22) - internal procedure

Owner: {{ROLE}} · inbox: {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} · deadline: **1 month** from receipt (+2 months if complex: tell the person
within the first month, with the reason).

1. **Log** the request (date, channel, person, right asked) in {{REGISTER}} - never the data itself.
2. **Identity**: check only when in doubt, proportionately (the account session is enough when signed in; no ID copy by default).
3. **Answer by right**
   - Access (art. 15): the export {{tool / endpoint}} + purposes, recipients, retention, source, rights.
   - Rectification (art. 16): correct in {{every system}}.
   - Erasure (art. 17): run {{the delete procedure}}: database rows and files, caches and search index, processors
     ({{list with their deletion API / form}}), mailing lists; backups expire after {{N}} days (say it); keep only
     what a law requires (invoices), restricted.
   - Restriction (art. 18) / objection (art. 21: marketing - stop at once) / portability (art. 20: JSON / CSV of what
     they gave) / human review of an automated decision (art. 22).
   - Tell the recipients of a rectification / erasure / restriction (art. 19).
4. **Refusal** only for a manifestly unfounded or excessive request, or a legal exception: say why and the right to
   complain to the CNIL and to go to court.
5. **Close**: date answered, what was done, in the register.
