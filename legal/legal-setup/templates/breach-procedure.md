# Personal data breach - procedure and register (GDPR art. 33-34)

A breach: personal data destroyed, lost, altered, disclosed or accessed without authorisation (a leak, a lost laptop,
an email to the wrong person, ransomware, a public bucket).

1. **Contain** at once: cut the access, rotate the secrets, keep the evidence (logs).
2. **Assess** (within hours): what data, how many people, consequences, likely risk to them.
3. **Notify the authority within 72 h** of becoming aware (CNIL: online notification service) unless the breach is
   unlikely to result in a risk; late: say why. Details may follow in steps.
4. **Tell the people** without undue delay when the risk is **high** (what happened, the likely consequences, the
   measures, what they should do, a contact).
5. **Processor** of a client: tell the client without undue delay (art. 33(2)), they notify.
6. **Register** every breach, even not notified: date, facts, data and people, consequences, measures, notified or
   not and why.
7. **Product / software** sold in the EU: actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents also to the CSIRT /
   ENISA platform under the Cyber Resilience Act (24 h / 72 h / final report) from 11 Sept 2026.
